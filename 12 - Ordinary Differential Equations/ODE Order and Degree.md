## Core Idea

The order of an ODE is set by the highest derivative of the unknown function $y$, and its degree is the highest power of that highest derivative.

## Key Equation

$$
\text{order}=\text{highest derivative of } y,\qquad \text{degree}=\text{highest power of that highest derivative}
$$

**Derived From:** definition (the source lists these as classification of the ODE, followed by worked examples whose content is not in the transcribed text).

## Supporting Formulas

- Order: determined by the highest derivative of $y$ (the unknown function).
- Degree: the highest power of the highest derivative.

## Derivation Outline

1. Find the highest derivative of $y$ that appears — this is the order.
2. Find the power to which that derivative is raised — this is the degree.
