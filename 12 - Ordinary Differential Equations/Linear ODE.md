## Core Idea

A linear ODE of order $n$ is one that is built from a linear combination of the unknown function and its derivatives plus a function of $x$; it is homogeneous when that extra function is zero.

## Key Equation

$$
F\left(x,y,y',y'',\dots,y^{(n)}\right)=\alpha_0y+\alpha_1y'+\alpha_2y''+\cdots+\alpha_ny^{(n)}+f(x)
$$

**Derived From:** definition, as written in the source. If $f(x)=0$ the ODE is called homogeneous.

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ f(x)=0 $$ — the ODE is homogeneous.
- $n$ is the [order](../12%20-%20Ordinary%20Differential%20Equations/ODE%20Order%20and%20Degree.md) of the ODE.

## Derivation Outline

1. Write the ODE as a linear combination of $y,y',\dots,y^{(n)}$ plus $f(x)$.
2. Check whether $f(x)=0$ (homogeneous) or not (non-homogeneous).
