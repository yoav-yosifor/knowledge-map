## Core Idea

An ODE whose right-hand side factors into a function of $x$ times a function of $y$ can be solved by moving all $y$ terms to one side and all $x$ terms to the other, then integrating both sides.

## Key Equation

$$
y'=f(x)\,g(y)
$$

**Derived From:** definition — an ODE that can be written in this form is called a separable ODE.

## Supporting Formulas

- Integrating both sides after separating gives the **general solution**.
- Using an initial condition to fix the constant gives a [particular solution](../12%20-%20Ordinary%20Differential%20Equations/General%20and%20Particular%20Solutions.md).

## Derivation Outline

1. Replace the derivative notation: $y'=\dfrac{dy}{dx}$.
2. Move terms so that one side has only $y$ and the other side has only $x$.
3. Integrate both sides.
4. Use the initial condition, if one is given, to find the constant.
