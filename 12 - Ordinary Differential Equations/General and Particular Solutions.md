## Core Idea

The general solution of an ODE is the whole infinite family of functions that satisfy it, while a particular solution is the single member of that family picked out by an initial condition.

## Key Equation

$$
y(t)=f(t)+c
$$

**Derived From:** the source's example form. The general solution always contains an integration constant $c$; replacing $c$ with a specific number, using an initial (anchor) condition, gives a particular solution.

## Supporting Formulas

- **General solution:** a family of infinitely many solutions; represents all functions that satisfy the differential equation; always contains an integration constant $c$.
- **Particular solution:** one defined solution; represents one specific scenario out of infinitely many possibilities; the constant $c$ is replaced by an exact number using an initial condition.

## Derivation Outline

1. Solve the ODE — the result contains the integration constant $c$ (general solution).
2. Plug in the initial condition.
3. Solve for $c$ to get the particular solution.
