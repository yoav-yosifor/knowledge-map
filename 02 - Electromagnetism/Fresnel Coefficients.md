## Core Idea

At a planar interface between two dielectric media, the reflected and transmitted field amplitudes depend on polarization (TE vs. TM) and on the angles fixed by Snell's law. These ratios of reflected/transmitted to incident amplitude are the Fresnel coefficients.

## Key Equation

$$
r_{TE} = \frac{n_1\cos\theta_{in} - n_2\cos\theta_t}{n_1\cos\theta_{in} + n_2\cos\theta_t}
$$

**Derived From:** stated directly in the source alongside Snell's law $n_1\sin\theta_{in}=n_2\sin\theta_t$ and $\theta_{in}=\theta_r$; no derivation from the boundary conditions was shown in the material.

## Supporting Formulas

- $t_{TE} = \dfrac{2n_1\cos\theta_{in}}{n_1\cos\theta_{in} + n_2\cos\theta_t}$
- $r_{TM} = \dfrac{n_2\cos\theta_{in} - n_1\cos\theta_t}{n_2\cos\theta_{in} + n_1\cos\theta_t}$
- $t_{TM} = \dfrac{2n_1\cos\theta_{in}}{n_2\cos\theta_{in} + n_1\cos\theta_t}$

## Derivation Outline

1. Write the incident, reflected, and transmitted plane waves on each side of the interface.
2. Apply Snell's law to fix the transmitted angle $\theta_t$ in terms of the incident angle $\theta_{in}$.
3. Impose continuity of tangential $E$ and $H$ at the interface, separately for TE and TM polarization.
4. Solve the resulting linear equations for $r$ and $t$ in each case.
