## Entry: 2026-07-29-su2-su11-vs-bloch-sphere

**System A:** [SU(2) and SU(1,1) Dynamics](SU(2)%20and%20SU(1,1)%20Dynamics.md) - Quantum Optics course notes — 16 - Non-Linear Optics and the Generation of Squeezed Light
**System B:** [Bloch Sphere](../10%20-%20Quantum%20Information/Bloch%20Sphere.md) - Quantum Information course notes — 02 - Single Qubit System
**Shared structure:**

```
SU(2): the compact rotation group, representable as ordinary rotations on a sphere.
Linear optics (photon-number-conserving phase shifters and beamsplitters) and
single-qubit unitaries (Bloch-sphere rotations) are both representations of this
same SU(2) algebra.
```

**Why it matters:** The reason a single qubit's state always lives on (or inside) a sphere, and the reason linear optics can only ever rotate or redistribute photons without amplifying them, are the same fact stated in two different physical languages — both are the compact group SU(2). Intuition built for one (rotation axes, geodesics, Bloch-vector precession) transfers directly to the other. It also sharpens the contrast with SU(1,1): nothing in the qubit/Bloch-sphere picture has a natural SU(1,1) analogue, since a two-level system has no "pair creation" operation the way bosonic modes do — squeezing has no qubit counterpart.
