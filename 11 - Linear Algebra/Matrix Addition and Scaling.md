## Core Idea

Matrices of the same size are added entry by entry, and a scalar multiplies every entry.

## Key Equation

$$
(A+B)_{ij}=(A)_{ij}+(B)_{ij}\ \ \forall\, i,j,\qquad (\alpha A)_{ij}=\alpha\,(A)_{ij}
$$

**Derived From:** definition, for $A,B\in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ and scalars $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{F}$. Addition is defined only for matrices of the same size.

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ A+B=B+A $$ — commutativity of addition
- $$ \alpha(A+B)=\alpha A+\alpha B $$ — scalar distributes over matrix addition
- $$ (\alpha+\beta)A=\alpha A+\beta A $$ — matrix distributes over scalar addition

## Derivation Outline

1. Check that the two matrices have the same size.
2. Add (or scale) each entry separately.
3. Properties follow entry by entry from the field properties of $\mathbb{F}$.
