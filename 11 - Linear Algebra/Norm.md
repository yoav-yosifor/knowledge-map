## Core Idea

A norm is a function $\|\cdot\|:V\to\mathbb{R}$ on a vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$ that assigns each vector a non-negative length and obeys positivity, absolute homogeneity, and the triangle inequality; every inner product induces one.

## Key Equation

*The three norm axioms, for all $\alpha\in\mathbb{F}$ and $v,u\in V$:*

$$
\|v\|\ge 0\ \ (\text{equality iff } v=0),\qquad \|\alpha v\|=|\alpha|\,\|v\|,\qquad \|v+u\|\le\|v\|+\|u\|
$$

**Derived From:** definition of a norm on a vector space $V$ over $\mathbb{F}$ — non-negativity with equality only at $0$, absolute homogeneity, triangle inequality.

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ \|v\|:=\sqrt{\langle v,v\rangle} $$ — the norm induced by an inner product on an inner product space $V$
- $$ \|v+u\|\le\|v\|+\|u\| $$ — triangle inequality (axiom 3)
