## Core Idea

In any inner product space, the absolute value of the inner product of two vectors is bounded by the product of their norms.

## Key Equation

$$
|\langle x,y\rangle|\le\|x\|\cdot\|y\|\qquad\forall\,x,y\in V
$$

**Derived From:** stated as a theorem in the source, with $\|\cdot\|$ the norm induced by the inner product; no proof is given there.
