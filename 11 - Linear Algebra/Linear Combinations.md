## Core Idea

A linear combination of vectors is a sum of scalar multiples of them.

## Key Equation

$$
\alpha_1v_1+\alpha_2v_2+\cdots+\alpha_nv_n,\qquad \alpha_i\in\mathbb{F},\ v_i\in V
$$

**Derived From:** definition, for $V$ a vector space over $\mathbb{F}$.

## Supporting Formulas

- A linear combination in which $\alpha_i=0$ for every $i$ is called the **trivial** linear combination.

## Derivation Outline

1. Pick vectors $v_1,\dots,v_n\in V$ and scalars $\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n\in\mathbb{F}$.
2. Scale each vector, then add — the result is again in $V$ by closure.
