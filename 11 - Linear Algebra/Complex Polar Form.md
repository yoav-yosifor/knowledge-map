## Core Idea

Switching from Cartesian to polar coordinates in the complex plane writes every complex number as a radius times a direction given by an angle measured counter-clockwise from the positive $x$ axis.

## Key Equation

$$
(a,b)\leftrightarrow a+bi\leftrightarrow r\cos\theta+i\,r\sin\theta=r\cdot\operatorname{cis}(\theta)
$$

**Derived From:** the vector representation of $\mathbb{C}$ in the plane, transformed from Cartesian to polar coordinates.

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ \operatorname{cis}(\theta):=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta $$ — shorthand for "**c**os + **i**·**s**in"
- $$ 0\le r\in\mathbb{R} $$ — the radius is a non-negative real
- $$ 0\le\theta<2\pi $$ — the angle (argument), measured from the positive $x$ axis, counter-clockwise
- $$ z_1=z_2 \iff r_1=r_2,\ \theta_1=\theta_2 $$ — equality in polar form
