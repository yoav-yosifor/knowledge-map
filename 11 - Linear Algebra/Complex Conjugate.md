## Core Idea

The complex conjugate of a complex number flips the sign of its imaginary part.

## Key Equation

$$
\bar z := a-bi \qquad (z=a+bi)
$$

**Derived From:** definition; e.g. $\overline{2-4i}=2+4i$.

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ \overline{(\bar z)}=z $$ — conjugating twice returns the original
- $$ \overline{z_1+z_2}=\overline{z_1}+\overline{z_2} $$ — conjugation distributes over addition
- $$ \overline{z_1\cdot z_2}=\overline{z_1}\cdot\overline{z_2} $$ — conjugation distributes over multiplication
- $$ z+\bar z=2\operatorname{Re}(z) $$ —
- $$ z-\bar z=2i\operatorname{Im}(z) $$ — since $(a+bi)-(a-bi)=2bi$
- $$ z\cdot\bar z=(a+bi)(a-bi)=a^2+b^2\in\mathbb{R} $$ — the product with the conjugate is real
- $$ z\in\mathbb{R}\iff z=\bar z $$ — a number is real iff it equals its conjugate
