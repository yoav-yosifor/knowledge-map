## Core Idea

The transpose flips a matrix across its main diagonal, swapping rows and columns.

## Key Equation

$$
A\in\mathbb{F}^{m\times n},\qquad (A^t)_{i,j}=A_{j,i}
$$

**Derived From:** definition.

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ (AB)^t=B^tA^t $$ — when the product is defined (order reverses)
- $$ (A^t)^t=A $$
- $$ (A+B)^t=A^t+B^t $$ — for matrices of the same size
- $$ (\alpha A)^t=\alpha(A^t) $$ — for any scalar $\alpha$

## Derivation Outline

1. Swap the two indices of each entry.
2. The result is $n\times m$.
3. Properties follow entry by entry from the definition.
