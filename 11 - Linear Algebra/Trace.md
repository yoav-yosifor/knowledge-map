## Core Idea

The trace of a square matrix is the sum of its main-diagonal entries.

## Key Equation

$$
A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F}),\qquad \operatorname{tr}(A)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}A_{ii}
$$

**Derived From:** definition.

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ \operatorname{tr}(A+B)=\operatorname{tr}(A)+\operatorname{tr}(B) $$
- $$ \operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)\quad (A\in M_{m\times n},\ B\in M_{n\times m}) $$ — but in general $\operatorname{tr}(AB)\neq\operatorname{tr}(A)\operatorname{tr}(B)$
- $$ \operatorname{tr}(\alpha A)=\alpha\operatorname{tr}(A) $$
- $$ \operatorname{tr}(A)=\operatorname{tr}(A^t) $$

## Derivation Outline

1. Add the entries $A_{11},A_{22},\dots,A_{nn}$.
2. Properties follow from entrywise addition and scaling.
