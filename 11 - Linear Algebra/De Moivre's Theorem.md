## Core Idea

Multiplying two complex numbers in polar form means multiplying their radii and adding their angles; repeating this for a number with itself gives its powers.

## Key Equation

$$
z_1z_2=r_1r_2\operatorname{cis}(\theta_1+\theta_2) \qquad (z_1=r_1\operatorname{cis}\theta_1,\ z_2=r_2\operatorname{cis}\theta_2\in\mathbb{C})
$$

## Supporting Formulas

- $$ z^2=r^2\operatorname{cis}(2\theta) $$ — multiplying $z$ by itself
- $$ z^n=r^n\operatorname{cis}(n\theta) $$ — the general power
